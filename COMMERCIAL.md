# Commercial direction

The current release is the MIT community core. A commercial edition is planned; no paid product, price, availability date or support commitment is announced.

| Available in the MIT core | Possible future commercial extension |
| --- | --- |
| Local reviewer accounts and task-scoped authorization | Enterprise SSO and identity provisioning |
| Per-deployment policy, approvals and audit records | Central administration across multiple deployments |
| Exportable audit records and independent audit heads | Managed retention, search and organization-wide reporting |
| Deployment, readiness, backup and recovery tools | Managed hosting and contracted operations |
| Public issues, documentation and contributions | Contracted support with explicit service terms |

Future commercial packages would integrate through the core's documented client, tool-adapter and administration interfaces. Core enforcement, exact approval binding, local audit verification and self-hosting remain available in the public project.

For a non-sensitive feature request or expression of interest, open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/paulchum/agent-effect-gate/issues). Do not put customer data, credentials or private incident details in public issues. Security reports use the channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md#report-a-security-issue).
